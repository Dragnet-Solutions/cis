Questions

What is the CIS - Dragnet Study?

A 2026 benchmark study by the Chartered Institute of Stockbrokers, delivered with Dragnet Solutions Limited. It measures how Nigeria's stockbroking firms operate and how their investors experience them, and reads the two side by side.

Who can take part?

Stockbroking firms invited by CIS; individual investors, Nigerian institutional investors and institutional investors abroad who use a Nigerian stockbroking firm; and six market institutions by invitation: SEC, NGX, CSCS, FMDQ, NASD and LCFE.

When does the survey open and close?

The survey opens on Monday 9 November 2026 and closes on Friday 11 December 2026.

When will the results be published?

On Friday 15 January 2027. The industry report is public, and each participating firm receives its own private benchmark report.

Are my answers confidential?

Yes. Individual answers are reported only in aggregate, and no figure based on fewer than ten respondents is published. Firms never see their investors' answers, a firm's coordinator never sees colleagues' answers, and no firm receives another named firm's results.

How does my firm take part?

CIS sends your firm's coordinator a personal invitation link. The coordinator sets up the firm's space with a six-digit PIN and names three respondents: the Managing Director or CEO, the Head of Compliance and the Head of Operations. Each receives their own survey link.

I am an investor. How do I start?

Use the link your stockbroking firm sends you, or start here. Your answers save as you go, and if you give an email address you receive a link to come back and finish. At the end you can ask to receive the published report.

Who do I contact for help?

Email the study team at support@cisdragnetstudy.com.
